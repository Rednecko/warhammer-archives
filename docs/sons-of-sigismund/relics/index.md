# Reliquary of the Sons of Sigismund

<div class="archive-page-label">Sacred objects · The first two relics</div>

<span class="archive-status">Founding artifacts established</span> <span class="archive-status open">Formal names & provenance open</span>

In the Sons of Sigismund, relics are not simply armaments of prestige. They are tangible fragments of Imperial memory and the sacrifices of the faithful. The order exists partly because its founding Marshal showed, on Armageddon, what such memory could achieve when carried into battle.

## The founding sword

A historically significant **relic sword**, recovered by the future Marshal during the Armageddon Ork campaign. He bore it through the long effort to rally the defenders and wielded it in a hard-fought **public duel against an original regional Ork Warboss**.

Its formal name, age, former bearers, exact type and acquisition circumstances remain undecided. It is not assumed to possess supernatural powers.

## The founding standard

An **ancient battle standard**, recovered during the same campaign. It became the visible sign around which the initial defenders rallied and later served as a symbol of the wider movement. It is intended to survive into the present-day household, borne by a **Crusader Ancient** in the miniature collection.

Its title, history, heraldic imagery, exact bearer and documented lineage have not been settled.

<div class="archive-quote">To preserve a relic is to remember a life. To carry it into battle is to make that memory a promise.</div>

## More than two artifacts

Sword Brethren may be entrusted with crosses, devotional relics, censers, ancestral arms, fragments of banners and other items as the collection and lore grow. Each needs an individual verified history before being presented as an established object.
