# Relics and reliquaries

The household's mission is to **recover, authenticate, preserve and defend** Imperial relics, archives and sacred sites while pursuing the Eternal Crusade.

## The founding sword

A historically significant relic sword recovered by the future Marshal during the Armageddon Ork campaign. He used it to defeat a significant regional Warboss in a hard-won public duel. Its name, provenance and exact properties are undecided.

## The founding standard

An ancient battle standard recovered alongside the sword. Raised at a desperate defensive position, it became a visible rallying point. The standard survives into the current order and will be represented by the user's **Crusader Ancient** miniature. Its bearer and provenance remain undecided.

These relics are symbols of shared sacrifice and Imperial memory, not automatically supernatural objects.
