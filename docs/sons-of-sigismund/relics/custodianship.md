# The sacred burden of custodianship

<div class="archive-page-label">Reliquary protocol · Living archive</div>

In the Sons of Sigismund, promotion to the Sword Brethren also brings a sacred duty: **to remember, study and safeguard the relics entrusted to the household**.

This is distinct from the Oath of Allegiance that makes a Black Templar permanently sworn to the order.

## Becoming a custodian

A newly recognized Sword Brother may be introduced to the reliquary, instructed in the history of a fitting artifact and entrusted with responsibility for its protection. The Chaplaincy oversees its spiritual obligations; technical specialists support conservation, testing and safe storage.

A warrior may carry more than one relic. Each new burden carries its own instruction and vow. **The working title is Oath of Custodianship; the final text has not been adopted.**

## The record that follows an artifact

A proper register may include:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Stable register identifier | Never reuse an ID for a different artifact |
| Object name and description | Distinguish a weapon, standard, badge, bone pendant, text or other object |
| Provenance | Where it was recovered; who owned it; what is *verified* rather than assumed |
| Condition and hazards | Conservation state; purity and technical examination as needed |
| Custodian and oath | Current bearer and history of prior custodians |
| Campaign record | Only recorded battle appearances, damage, recovery or losses |
| Current location | On campaign, in archives, under study, or in secured vault storage |

## Relic journey aboard ship

1. **Recovery and controlled reception** in the hangar/logistics complex.
2. **Quarantine, authentication and examination** beside the forge and archive support spaces.
3. **Spiritual and historical assessment** by the Chaplaincy and scribes.
4. **Controlled placement** in a collection, archive or the High Relic Vault—or renewed deployment under a sworn custodian.

This is the agreed *workflow concept*, not a final security manual.

## Remembering a fallen bearer

The Hall of Vigil serves as a place for lying in state and remembrance. A deceased brother's relics can be recovered, inspected, returned to the household and later entrusted to a new bearer. His name and his stewardship should remain part of the record.

## First confirmed founding objects

The [founding sword and standard](index.md) are the first agreed named-by-role artifacts. Their **formal names and provenance are still open**.

A four-object model-based register in an older working dossier (cross, torn banner, censer, bone pendant) is preserved in source notes as provisional material, **not** confirmed founding relics or an approved named custodian.
