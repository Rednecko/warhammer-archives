# Mandate and doctrine

<div class="archive-page-label">Order identity · Founding commission</div>

<div class="archive-quote">Memory is not a treasure to be hidden from war. It is a duty carried into war.</div>

The Sons of Sigismund are first and always **Black Templars**. Their specialization does not exempt them from the Eternal Crusade. High Marshal Helbrecht grants the founding Marshal a particular charge: seek endangered fragments of Imperial memory, preserve what can be saved, and make those relics a living source of resolve for the faithful.

## Four duties of the household

### I. Continue the Eternal Crusade

The household remains an active, hard-fighting crusading force. It may enter battle to reinforce an Imperial position, defeat an enemy of the Emperor or support another Black Templars crusade. Custodianship is a second responsibility, not a substitute for combat.

### II. Recover and protect

The order seeks historically or spiritually significant weapons, standards, chronicles, artifacts and shrines. Its mission includes authentication, conservation, record-keeping and defense. **The value of an object is not measured solely by its combat power.**

### III. Give memory back to the faithful

The founding Marshal's success on Armageddon established the order's central conviction: a standard, a recovered name, or the testimony of the fallen can restore courage. The Chaplaincy, Sword Brethren and the warriors themselves bring these histories to defenders in need. This does not mean one relic magically guarantees victory.

### IV. Remember sacrifice without denying its cost

A dead Guardsman's testimony may matter as much to the archive as a celebrated Astartes relic. The household values faithful service in Imperial soldiers and civilians, while remaining stern in matters of cowardice, betrayal and broken oaths.

## Principles of conduct

- **Lead by example.** The Marshal and his brothers share the dangers imposed upon allied defenders.
- **Preserve without abandoning judgment.** Relics must be protected, but a tactically justified withdrawal is not the same as dishonor.
- **Honor witnesses and bearers.** Provenance, previous custodians, and the context of a relic's recovery belong in the record.
- **Keep oaths distinct.** Swearing to the household, earning the title of Sword Brother and accepting an individual relic are separate obligations.
- **Remain Black Templars.** Their household has its own customs, but no separate Chapter Master, homeworld or independent gene-seed lineage.

## Strategic character

The order's small starting strength favors concentrated operations and close personal command. Smaller detachments may secure sites or reinforce other fronts, but the Marshal repeatedly fights with the same brothers rather than commanding a distant network of companies.

!!! info "Editorial distinction"
