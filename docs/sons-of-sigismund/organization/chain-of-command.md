# Order and chain of command

<div class="archive-page-label">Organization · Founding authority</div>

The Sons of Sigismund are a specialized **Black Templars household**. Their Marshal does not become an independent Chapter Master, and their ranks cannot be treated as a conventional Codex-compliant company structure.

<div class="archive-steps">
  <div class="archive-step"><h3>High Marshal Helbrecht</h3><p>Supreme external authority within the Black Templars; grants the founding mandate and can assign wider crusading duties.</p></div>
  <div class="archive-step"><h3>The Founding Marshal</h3><p>Directs strategy, deployments, battlefield operations and membership decisions within the household.</p></div>
  <div class="archive-step"><h3>Chaplaincy and command support</h3><p>Spiritual discipline, oaths and relic rites are led by the Chaplaincy; a Castellan role may support command. These do not displace the Marshal's operational authority.</p></div>
  <div class="archive-step"><h3>Sword Brethren and Crusader formations</h3><p>Veterans lead, mentor, fight and protect sacred artifacts. Initiates and Neophytes serve through the household's operational squads, whether permanently sworn or attached.</p></div>
  <div class="archive-step"><h3>Specialists, shipmaster and serfs</h3><p>Techmarines, Apothecaries, archives staff, mortal ship crew and support personnel sustain the ship and its mission. Exact founding appointments remain open.</p></div>
</div>

## Founding versus eventual strength

The **initial permanent veteran cadre** is ten Sword Brethren. The Marshal, Emperor's Champion and founding Chaplain are separate appointments. Two Crusader Squads serve initially **without permanent household oaths**.

Earlier proposals of approximately 60 permanent and 100 rotating Marines describe a potential *later* force and **do not describe the founding muster**.

The order can expand over months through attachments, transfers, volunteers and accepted oaths of allegiance.

## Close command

The Marshal habitually accompanies the main fighting force. The ship can support small detachments on neighboring battlefields or specialized recovery operations, but the order's core is not intended to fragment into numerous distant, autonomous companies.
