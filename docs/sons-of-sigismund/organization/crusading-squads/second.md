# 2.3.2 Second Crusading Squad

<div class="archive-page-label">Sons of Sigismund · Crusading Squad roster II</div>

<span class="archive-status">Second founding squad attachment established</span> <span class="archive-status open">Leadership and individual roster open</span>

The **Second Crusading Squad** is the **second attached Black Templars Crusader Squad** at the order's founding. Like its counterpart, it is a **small formation intended for roughly 10–30 Space Marines**, not a full Fighting Company. The range is a **working scale**, not its documented current headcount.

## Personnel register

| Position | Current record |
|---|---|
| Squad leader | **Not yet named or formally designated** |
| Crusader Squad | **One original attached squad**; individual Initiates and Neophytes unknown |
| Sword Brother assigned to the squad | **Not yet established** |
| Exact strength | **Open** |
| Formal squad name / personal heraldry | **Open** |

The squad's warriors are **initially attached, not yet permanently sworn**. We have not assigned it a dedicated Castellan, senior Sword Brother, support specialists or transport. The Marshal may allocate help temporarily according to the operation.

One of the original ten Sword Brethren is **reserved for a later Crusader Squad**. That does **not** automatically make him the Second Crusading Squad's leader. The separately designated household duelist also remains unassigned here.

## Developing this squad

As characters and models become established, we will add individual Initiates, Neophytes, mentors if chosen, equipment, oaths and recorded battle history. **No post-founding tabletop battle has been played.**
