# 2.3.3 Third Crusading Squad

<div class="archive-page-label">Sons of Sigismund · Future Crusading Squad formation</div>

<span class="archive-status open">Planned formation — not yet mustered</span>

The **Third Crusading Squad** is a **reserved place in the Sons of Sigismund's long-term four-squad structure**. It was **not** among the two attached Black Templars Crusader Squads present at the founding. Neither personnel nor battles have been invented for it.

## Intended 20-Marine roster

| Future slot | Planned strength | Current status |
|---|---:|---|
| Household Sword Brother assigned to the squad | **1** | Not yet selected |
| Initiates and Neophytes combined | **19** | Recruitment and exact mix open |
| **Squad total** | **20 Space Marines** | **Target only — no active roster** |

The Sword Brother will **remain a member of the Marshal's Household**, serving this squad on assignment rather than creating an independent veteran unit. No separate Castellan, permanent support staff, Apothecary, Techmarine or vehicle pool is assumed.

## Details to develop

The personal squad name, individual brother names, exact Initiate-to-Neophyte proportions, battlefield duties and future campaign history will be recorded only as they are established. There is **no founding roster and no post-founding battle history** for this squad.
