## A reserve is not automatically an assignment

One of the founding ten Sword Brethren is **reserved for a later Crusader Squad**. That does **not automatically make him commander of this existing second attachment**. The assignment may be decided later, as the order and collection develop. The designated household duelist likewise remains separate unless explicitly transferred.

## Future development

This will become the second squad's roster, with individual Initiates and Neophytes, a named formation leader if appointed, model equipment, heraldry and played battle history. The squad's original **temporary attachment** remains part of its history even if its warriors later swear to the Sons.
