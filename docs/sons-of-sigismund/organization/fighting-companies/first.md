# 2.3.1 First Fighting Company

<div class="archive-page-label">Sons of Sigismund · Company dossier I (working designation)</div>

**First Fighting Company** is a **working name** for the small formation based on the **first attached Crusader Squad**. The two founding Sword Brethren assigned to serve alongside that squad are the only established permanent veteran support in this formation. It is **not** an independent 100-warrior company.

## Active and rotating affiliation

The **attached Crusader Squad is not permanently sworn** to the Sons of Sigismund at founding. The two Sword Brethren belong to the household's permanent founding cadre. A company's operational roster thus can include warriors with **different allegiance status**.
