In this wiki **Fighting Company** is our **working organizational label** for a small crusading formation built around a **Crusader Squad**. This follows the presentation of the reference diagram without copying its scale. The Sons of Sigismund **do not** have four large company-sized armies or a Codex-style company structure at their founding.

At their first muster, **two Crusader Squads serve on attachment** to the newly commissioned household. We are organizing those under **First Fighting Company** and **Second Fighting Company** as **provisional headings** until we choose formal names and confirm how these forces are commanded.

## What is a Fighting Company here?

A **small operational grouping**, not an assumption of one hundred Space Marines. At this stage a Fighting Company is **one attached Crusader Squad**, with an officer or veteran supervising it and any support assigned by the Marshal as the story and collection develop. Neither the company designation nor the supporting equipment proves permanent household membership.

| Property | At the first muster |
|---|---|
| Number of formations | **Two** |
| Minimum known core of each | **One attached Crusader Squad** |
| Actual Initiates and Neophytes | **Undecided** |
| Permanently sworn status of attached squads | **No** — temporary attachments |
| Dedicated vehicle and specialist support | **Undecided** |
| Permanent commander / Castellan | **Not assigned in current canon** |
| First post-founding deployments | **Not played or recorded** |

## Relationship to the Marshal's Household

The Marshal maintains command of both formations. The **Household is separate** from these two Fighting Companies. Its six personal guards are not divided into companies without a specific later assignment, and the **Emperor's Champion** is not automatically a company commander.

The founding cadre also contains a **household duelist** and a **Sword Brother reserved for a future Crusader Squad**; neither should be silently placed into one of these two attached companies.

We will populate these fields **as decisions are made**, without inventing squad sizes or turning reference art into our unit roster.
