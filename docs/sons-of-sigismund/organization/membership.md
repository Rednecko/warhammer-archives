# 2.2 Recruitment, Membership and Advancement

<div class="archive-page-label">Chapter Organization · How warriors enter and advance</div>

The **Black Templars Chapter** recruits and develops Space Marines; the **Sons of Sigismund household** separately determines which of those warriors serve its own Marshal, whether temporarily or through permanent allegiance. Those are **two different processes**. The historical identity of the first members is recorded under [Chapter Origins](../history/founding.md#the-cruiser-and-first-muster), not duplicated here.

## 2.2.1 Aspirants and Neophytes

The Sons of Sigismund have **no independent recruitment world, successor-Chapter gene-seed or approved household-specific aspirant trial**. Their members are Black Templars, descended through the Imperial Fists line of Rogal Dorn.

Within the wider Black Templars tradition, aspirants become **Neophytes**, apprenticed to experienced warriors, and then **Initiates** as they complete their development. The precise recruitment worlds, original aspirant-selection details, gene-seed rites and any special recruitment authority for this **particular household** are **open**.

This process **must not be mistaken** for a veteran of an existing Black Templars crusade transferring into the Sons.

## 2.2.2 Temporary attachment and transfer

A Black Templar may be assigned to the Sons from another crusade for a campaign, mission or longer service. He remains part of the wider Chapter and does **not automatically become a permanent Son of Sigismund**. He may later return to his former crusade or request acceptance into the household.

The conditions of initial assignment and the chain of approvals are not yet fully specified.

## 2.2.3 Permanent allegiance

A brother accepted for permanent membership takes an **Oath of Allegiance** to the Sons of Sigismund. This determines **household affiliation, not military rank**. Exact vows, witnesses and entrance ceremonies remain open.

| Relationship | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Temporary attachment | Serves with the household while retaining affiliation to another Black Templars crusade |
| Newly accepted permanent member | Takes the household's Oath of Allegiance |
| Transfer of an existing veteran | May retain veteran standing while joining this household |
| Advancement within the household | Changes a warrior's status or responsibilities; does not by itself create a separate Chapter lineage |

## 2.2.4 Advancement through the ranks

  <div class="archive-step"><h3>Aspirant → Neophyte</h3><p>Development within the parent Black Templars; any unique local screening remains open.</p></div>
  <div class="archive-step"><h3>Neophyte → Initiate</h3><p>Completion of the Black Templars' path to full battle-brother standing; the household's particular ceremony is not established.</p></div>
  <div class="archive-step"><h3>Initiate → Sword Brother</h3><p>The household's established Trial of Swords involves mentorship in arms, faith and history and formal duels with veteran Sword Brethren. On induction, the warrior earns the lifelong, optional heraldic privilege of yellow.</p></div>
  <div class="archive-step"><h3>Veteran → Command or specialist office</h3><p>Further authority depends on individual appointment, calling and service. No fixed progression to Chaplain, Emperor's Champion, Castellan or Marshal is being invented.</p></div>
</div>

**The Oath of Allegiance**, **Trial of Swords**, and proposed **relic-specific Oath of Custodianship** are three separate commitments. A Sword Brother who transfers may already have earned his veteran distinction.

## 2.2.5 Personnel records

An eventual administrative register should distinguish **rank**, **current office**, **assigned formation**, **permanent versus attached affiliation**, **former crusade**, **mentor/apprentices**, and an individual **biography link**. This prevents a historical founding roster from being confused with the current organization.
