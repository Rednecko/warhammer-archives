# 2.7 Shared Support and Specialist Services

<div class="archive-page-label">Sons of Sigismund · Central services and battlefield assignments</div>

<span class="archive-status">Organizational principles established</span> <span class="archive-status open">Most personal appointments remain open</span>

The Sons of Sigismund are a **small crusading household within the Black Templars**, not a collection of self-sufficient companies. Their intended **four Crusading Squads of twenty Space Marines** draw on **shared household and shipboard resources**, under the Marshal and his Castellan. A warrior serving alongside a squad does **not** automatically become a permanently attached member of that squad.

This page is a **responsibilities and assignment register**. It does not create unapproved Marines, vehicles, officers or ship-crew numbers. Historic founding strength and the original allocation of Sword Brethren are recorded in [Chapter Origins](../history/founding.md#the-cruiser-and-first-muster).

## 2.7.1 Who holds authority?

| Function | Primary authority or relationship | Established detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Household command** | Marshal, assisted by Castellan | Castellan is the agreed deputy **rank/office**; individual bearer and date of appointment open |
| **Spiritual affairs** | Chaplaincy | **One founding Chaplain** established; controls rites and counsel within the household |
| **Sacred champion's duties** | Emperor's Champion | **One founding Champion**; not a general-purpose second-in-command |
| **Veteran mentorship** | Sword Brethren of the Marshal's Household | Ten at founding; veterans may serve beside Crusading Squads without leaving the Household |
| **Voidship operations** | Shipmaster and regular ship's command, under the Marshal's wider authority | The precise Shipmaster and crew organization remain open |

**A rank is not the same thing as an office or assignment.** The Sword Brother in a Crusading Squad is simultaneously a veteran within the Marshal's Household and a leader or mentor on field duty. Neither the Chaplain nor the Emperor's Champion is silently made a Castellan.

## 2.7.2 Specialists and services

| Service | Purpose in this household | Current personnel status |
|---|---|---|
| **Apothecarion** | Treatment of Space Marines and stewardship of gene-seed, including the wounded and fallen | Facilities in ship planning; **named Apothecary and complement unconfirmed** |
| **Forge and Techmarine service** | Repairs to weapons, armor, vehicles, and technical examination of recovered objects | Forge facilities planned; **specific Techmarines and appointments open** |
| **Reliquary custodians** | Intake, identification, documentation, quarantine where appropriate, preservation and transfer of recovered artifacts | The duty is **established**; bearer, archival office titles and staffing open |
| **Chaplaincy and remembrance** | Religious rites, remembrance of sacrifice, oaths and spiritual examination of relic custody | One founding Chaplain **established**; expanded chaplaincy staffing open |
| **Standard bearer / Crusade Ancient** | Ceremonial and battlefield care of the founding battle standard | **Working miniature role**; the banner's formal bearer and assignment are not approved |
| **Mortal bridge and ship crew** | Navigation, communications, engineering, maintenance, supply and watchkeeping aboard *The Righteous Crusader* | Necessary ship functions; **shipmaster and crew numbers unconfirmed** |
| **Armories and transport pool** | Store and issue weapons, ammunition, supplies, land vehicles and assault craft as available | Ship functions planned; **no finalized vehicle-by-vehicle order of battle** |

A proposed **High Chaplain in Terminator armor** is an *individual character concept*, not evidence that multiple new Chaplains joined at founding. Likewise, a planned apothecarion or forge does **not** mean a named Apothecary or Techmarine was present in the original ceremony.

## 2.7.3 Allocation to the Crusading Squads

At full intended strength, the field organization is **four squads of twenty**—each with **one Household Sword Brother plus nineteen Initiates and Neophytes**. The actual balance of Initiates and Neophytes is still undecided. The first two squads were originally attached from the Black Templars; the Third and Fourth are **future formations**.

A mission may place a Chaplain, Champion, Apothecary, Techmarine, transport or several Household veterans alongside a particular squad. This is a **temporary or purpose-specific assignment**, unless the roster explicitly says otherwise.

The First Crusading Squad's **two founding Sword Brethren** are a historical assignment; they cannot be reduced retroactively to one just because one per squad is the long-term plan. The six founding honor guards, household duelist and reserve Sword Brother remain part of the **same original ten**, not additional squads or a second veteran establishment.

## 2.7.4 Equipment and supply record

The real equipment inventory will be developed as models and specific lore are approved. This is a tracking framework, **not an assertion that the resources already exist**.

| Equipment or resource | What remains to be entered |
|---|---|
| Individual wargear | Named bearers, weapon patterns, approved marks and relic histories |
| Ground vehicles | Model/type, name if any, assigned crew, custody and whether temporarily allocated |
| Dreadnoughts | Occupant and history **only when specifically approved** |
| Assault and landing craft | Actual craft complement versus provisional ship hangar capacity |
| Relics | Name, provenance, custodian, examination record and recorded deployment |
| Supplies | Where carried, who issues them and how resupply is handled |

An entry in the [flagship's deck guide](../fleet/deck-guide.md) describing **possible capacity** is not automatically an entry in this unit's active military inventory.

## 2.7.5 Decisions still needed

The following are **open**, not obstacles to presenting the structure: the Castellan's identity and date of appointment; first named Apothecary and Techmarine (if appointed); the banner's bearer; how relic examination is shared by Chaplaincy, forge and archivists; mortal shipmaster and serf relationships; the vehicle register; and whether temporary support is organized through set battlefield protocols.

The core principle stays simple: **one command household, four eventual small Crusading Squads, and specialists assigned according to mission—not four mini-companies with duplicate staffs.**
