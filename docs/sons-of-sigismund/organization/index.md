# 2. Chapter Organization

<div class="archive-page-label">Sons of Sigismund · A small Black Templars household</div>

The Sons of Sigismund begin as a **single Marshal's Household and two small Fighting Companies**, not a network of fully staffed, hundred-warrior companies. Their structure is built around people who already know one another, support one another in battle and belong to a single, compact crusading order.

The **Fighting Companies** are our working name for the formations built around the two **attached Crusader Squads** at founding. These names help us build the roster now; their formal titles, leaders and actual squad sizes remain **open**.
