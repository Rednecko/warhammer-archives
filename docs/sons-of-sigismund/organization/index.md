# 2. Chapter Organization

<div class="archive-page-label">Sons of Sigismund · A compact crusading household</div>

The Sons of Sigismund are a **small Black Templars crusading household** led by their Marshal and a **Castellan as second-in-command**. Their veteran Sword Brethren remain part of the **Marshal's Household**, even when assigned as fighting-squad mentors or battlefield leaders.

The **intended long-term establishment is four Crusading Squads of 20 Space Marines each**: **one Sword Brother plus 19 Initiates and Neophytes per squad**. This is a future organizational target, **not an existing 80-Marine muster**. The proportion of Initiates to Neophytes and each squad's personal name are still open. No squad automatically receives its own Castellan, Apothecary, Techmarine or standing vehicle detachment.

**At the founding, only two Black Templars Crusader Squads were attached** to the new order. The First and Second Crusading Squads represent those same two formations; the Third and Fourth are reserved positions for later expansion. The founding ten Sword Brethren are not multiplied to populate this target structure.
