# 2. Chapter Organization

<div class="archive-page-label">Sons of Sigismund · Ranks, structure, membership, insignia and battle dress</div>

The Sons of Sigismund are **a crusading household of the Black Templars**, not a newly established Space Marine Chapter. In this encyclopedia, **Chapter Organization** is the familiar wiki heading used to describe the household's actual chain of command, the service relationships of its warriors, and how they are identified on the battlefield.

This section is a **working reference for how the order operates**, rather than a second telling of its founding. The initial roster, appointments and ceremony remain in [Chapter Origins](../history/founding.md#the-cruiser-and-first-muster).

## 2.1 Command and military responsibility

**High Marshal Helbrecht** commands the wider Black Templars and remains a higher authority outside the Sons' internal command. Their **Marshal** directs the household and its operations. The **Chaplaincy** manages spiritual rites and discipline; an **Emperor's Champion** fulfills a distinct sacred combat calling rather than serving as an ordinary deputy.

**Sword Brethren** are veterans who can serve as personal guards, mentors and combat leaders. **Initiates** are full Astartes battle-brothers; **Neophytes** remain their apprentices. A **Castellan** is an anticipated but not yet confirmed household command appointment. Technical, medical and shipboard offices support the fighting force without being treated as interchangeable battle ranks.

## 2.2 Recruitment and service

The Sons have **no independent recruiting homeworld**. New Marines are raised within the Black Templars' wider traditions; the **specific aspirant-recruiting system for this household is not yet established**. At the household level, other Black Templars may serve on attachment, transfer from another crusade or take a permanent **Oath of Allegiance**.

**Becoming a permanent Son**, **earning Sword Brother status**, and **taking custody of a relic** involve separate processes and vows.

## 2.3 Heraldry and distinctions

**Black armor, white shoulder guards and the Black Templars cross** remain central to the order. **Yellow armor accents are a personal, optional, lifelong honor earned by Sword Brethren**, evoking Sigismund and the Imperial Fists. No complete official-looking scheme for helmets, squad numbers or shoulder trim has been ratified.

[Read the heraldry register →](heraldry.md)

## 2.4 Armor and painting

The miniature collection uses **weathered, grimdark black armor rather than blue edge highlighting**, with pale shoulder plates and heavy battle wear. The practical painting notes are a **working studio method**, not an in-universe order that every marine must be painted identically.
