# Founding of the Sons of Sigismund

## I. The Sword Brother on Armageddon

Before his commission, the future Marshal was an accomplished veteran **Sword Brother** serving amid an Ork campaign on Armageddon. As Imperial positions deteriorated, he recovered an ancient battle standard and a historically important relic sword. Their names and origins remain undecided.

He raised the standard and rallied one endangered position, then spent weeks or months helping to rebuild resistance across the sector. His Initiates and Neophytes carried his example and messages along the trenches; Imperial officers, priests, vox crews and Guardsmen spread the movement further. It was not one speech or one man who won the campaign.

## II. The Warboss's challenge

Working with Imperial officers, the Sword Brother defeated lesser Ork bosses, provoked a significant **original regional Warboss** and deliberately drew him toward a highly visible open battlefield. The confrontation combined strategic bait with an open challenge. The Sword Brother fought with the relic sword, the standard displayed, and defeated the Warboss in a difficult public duel.

The Ork command faltered, but victory was not instantaneous. Imperial defenders sustained a counteroffensive and recovered a significant regional sector. The larger war for Armageddon continued.

## III. Helbrecht's commission

Accounts of the victory and the sustained restoration of Imperial morale reached **High Marshal Helbrecht**. The Sword Brother did not seek a promotion. Helbrecht summoned him privately, examined his judgment and motives, and recognized a warrior who credited his brothers and Imperial defenders rather than himself.

Helbrecht offered him command; a later formal ceremony elevated him to **Marshal** and established the **Sons of Sigismund** as a Black Templars crusading household. Their purpose joined the Eternal Crusade with the recovery, authentication, protection and preservation of Imperial relics and records.

## IV. Mustering the household

The existing strike cruiser [*The Righteous Crusader*](../fleet/righteous-crusader.md) was assigned and adapted as flagship, mobile fortress-monastery and reliquary in or near the Armageddon theater. Original companions and newly appointed veterans formed the founding cadre; two Crusader Squads served on initial attachment.

## V. The first crusade

The newly commissioned household prepares to fight in the continuing Armageddon theater. **It has no recorded post-founding battles yet.** The Marshal's Warboss duel belongs to his *pre-commission* history, not to the new order's tabletop battle log.
