# Life and movement aboard ship

<div class="archive-page-label">The Righteous Crusader · Operational walkthroughs</div>

The ship design is built around the idea that the sacred spaces, training spaces, combat areas and mortal services each have a defined purpose. These routes are **working concepts** preserved from the vessel's architecture draft.

## A brother called to battle

An Initiate leaves his alcove on the Brotherhood Deck, takes arms from his squad's dedicated armory and reports to his assigned departure point. He may reach the hangar gallery, a ready Drop Pod or a Thunderhawk through the Astartes deployment network without wandering through the Marshal's chambers.

For training he uses the squad sparring hall or the large arena below. For the Trial of Swords, a sponsored candidate is admitted to the **Hall of Blades** under formal supervision.

## A relic brought aboard

A recovered object arrives through controlled hangar reception, moves to the examination/quarantine suite and is registered, inspected and assessed. Spiritual significance, physical condition and provenance are separately considered. Only after authorization may it enter an ordinary collection, the archives or the **High Relic Vault**.

No recovered object passes directly from an open landing pad to the most secure vault.

## A wounded Imperial soldier

An allied aircraft uses its authorized landing area and casualty route to the **mortal medicae** facilities. The Space Marine Apothecarion remains nearby for Astartes but is not the default hospital for the mortal wounded.

## An allied commander at council

A visiting officer reaches the VIP/liaison space and, when invited, the **Grand Strategium** for operational planning. The visitor does not thereby gain access to the **Inner Sanctum**. The ship's regular bridge approach remains separate from the Marshal's private command lift.

## A sudden command emergency

The Marshal and officers may gather around the Strategium's hololith, enter the Reclusiam to address their warriors or take the secure command lift to the upper bridge. Meanwhile, the mortal Shipmaster's officers continue normal vessel operation through their own routes.

<div class="archive-strip"><strong>Design principle:</strong> The ship is a living fortress-monastery, not an open museum. Sacred memory must coexist with a working combat vessel, and security boundaries must remain meaningful.</div>
