# 7.8 Life, Movement and Access Aboard the Cruiser

<div class="archive-page-label">The Righteous Crusader · Cross-deck walkthroughs</div>

<span class="archive-status">Core circulation routes established in draft</span> <span class="archive-status open">Daily schedules, guard posts and exact lift locations open</span>

The ship's story works only if her rooms can be reached sensibly. *The Righteous Crusader* has one system for ordinary Astartes life, another for shipboard mortal crew, special pathways for commanders and dignitaries, and physically suitable freight, casualty, flight and relic-handling routes. Security is **layered**, but that does not mean the ship is inconvenient for its own warriors.

The following passages are drawn from the original ship design. They are **walkthroughs of the intended access system**, not a stamped blueprint or a newly established daily watch timetable.

## 7.8.1 A brother preparing for training

An Initiate leaves the semi-private alcoves of the [Brotherhood Deck](brotherhood.md), crosses the combined living area and enters the **equipment/hygiene passage**. From there he reaches the squad's dedicated **armory**, then the small adjoining **sparring hall** for daily exercises. For major training he uses the **squad-specific stairs or lift** to the independent **port or starboard main arena below**, while senior brothers and instructors supervise through upper training C&C galleries.

**Route:** Brotherhood alcove → equipment passage → armory → sparring room → dedicated arena approach → lower arena.

This route does **not** require passing through cargo loading yards or disturbing a religious duel in the Hall of Blades.

## 7.8.2 A brother called suddenly to battle

For deployment, the same Initiate arms himself, joins the main Astartes corridor and moves toward a **Brotherhood-elevation Thunderhawk berth** or the nearby **port/starboard Drop Pod gallery**. A short final prayer may be held among the relic alcoves before he boards. The planned **six ready Drop Pods** can launch before reserves are prepared; the total plan of twelve is not a claim that twelve are immediately ready. A Chaplain may address the deploying warriors from the **grand upper observation position** without sending them to the cargo holds.

**Route:** Brotherhood quarters → armory → main Astartes corridor → upper hangar/Drop Pod embarkation → aircraft or ready pod.

## 7.8.3 A recovered relic taken aboard

A recovered object passes first through **controlled lower hangar reception**, along a segregated cargo/relic pathway to the **secure examination complex adjoining the Techmarine forge** on the [Hangar/Logistics Deck](hangar-logistics.md). Its provenance and condition are logged. The Chaplaincy considers spiritual purity and significance; the Techmarine or qualified examiner studies materials and mechanisms. Hazardous or unresolved objects stay quarantined. Only authorized objects proceed upward to approved archives, lesser collections or the **High Relic Vault** of the [Sanctum](sanctum.md).

**Route:** Landing and secured reception → registered relic intake → quarantine/examination → approved archive or High Relic Vault (after authorization).

There is **no public hangar-to-High-Vault shortcut**. The precise shielded freight lift and guard details still need mapping.

## 7.8.4 A wounded Space Marine and a wounded Guardsman

An injured **Astartes** is received via the Astartes casualty route to the dedicated **Apothecarion**, where triage, surgery, recovery and protected gene-seed services are planned. An injured **Imperial Guardsman** brought by Valkyrie or other allied transport travels down the allied **dedicated ramp/lift and casualty corridor** into the **Mortal Medicae Wing** instead. This separation is practical medical organization, not a claim that mortal sacrifice matters less to the order.

**Astartes:** Hangar casualty reception → Astartes Apothecarion.

**Mortal ally:** Allied pad or hangar → dedicated casualty lift/ramp → Mortal Medicae.

Neither path must cross ordinary brotherhood beds, the Reclusiam or the Marshal's private rooms.

## 7.8.5 An allied commander invited to council

An Imperial officer arriving aboard proceeds through a **controlled VIP/liaison wing** on the [Sanctum level](sanctum.md). When summoned he enters the **Grand Strategium** for a holo-table military briefing. He does not gain passage through the **guarded central arch** into the Inner Sanctum. If authorized for ship-command duties he can use the **ordinary flanking command corridor** rising toward the [bridge](upper-command.md), **not** the Marshal's personal Strategium lift.

**Route:** Authorized arrival → VIP/liaison rooms → controlled Strategium meeting (if invited) → ordinary guest/command route.

## 7.8.6 The Marshal in an emergency

The Marshal may assemble his officers in the Strategium **behind the Grand Reclusiam**, study the hololith and send orders. He can then pass forward through the **paired guarded doors** into the chapel to address the brotherhood, rearward through the **guarded arch** to his private Inner Sanctum, or **upward through the dedicated command lift** to the bridge. The Shipmaster's mortal crew maintains vessel operations along its own access network meanwhile.

**Route choices:** Strategium → Reclusiam / Inner Sanctum / private command lift to bridge.

The **private Council Chamber** remains inside the Inner Sanctum; it is not the Strategium, bridge or training observation room.

## 7.8.7 A mortal crew member returning to duty

A permanent rating or technical worker uses the dedicated **mortal service corridors and lifts** from crew habitation to mess, workstations and, where authorized, the [deeper engineering regions](engineering.md). Bridge watch officers reach the bridge by their separate regular command approach. Human crew, including servitors, do **not** have to pass through sacred Astartes halls to keep the vessel functioning.

**Route:** Crew habitation/service region → crew lift network → assigned station or engineering watch.

The original ship archive does not define the rotation of watches, precise recreation rules or shift lengths; those remain **cultural and operational details to develop**.

## 7.8.8 A normal day in the fortress-monastery

The source does establish *what a day can contain*: devotional assembly at the **Grand Reclusiam**, private reading or shared conversation in the **Great Brotherhood Hall**, physical training in **sparring halls or the two arenas**, personal maintenance at the **squad armories**, and specialist work in the archives, relic examination and shipboard services. These spaces provide the stage for a lived brotherhood, without requiring us to fabricate a rigid hour-by-hour itinerary.

The Sword Brother supporting each future **Crusading Squad** is still a **member of the Marshal's Household**. His experience as a mentor or senior battlefield leader fits the training and social design of the deck, but actual roles and succession procedures remain open; the existing Black Templars rank structure is not being rewritten.

## 7.8.9 Access-control summary

| Boundary | Who passes | What it prevents |
|---|---|---|
| Reclusiam → Strategium | Authorized commanders and guests | Casual access to active military planning |
| Strategium → Inner Sanctum | Restricted Household personnel | Visitors entering private residences, archive and relic vault |
| Strategium → upper command lift | Authorized leadership | Ordinary bridge-watch traffic using the Marshal's personal route |
| VIP wings → ordinary bridge approach | Authorized mortal officers and visitors | Requiring ship's officers to cross the Reclusiam |
| Brotherhood complexes → arenas | Marines and authorized training support | Routine drills crossing cargo holds |
| Allied wings → hangar/mortal medicae | Allied formations | Allied traffic entering Astartes barracks |
| Hangar → relic processing → vault | Examined and authorized objects only | Unregistered artifacts entering sacred collections |
| Mortal crew service network | Permanent crew and approved workers | Daily industrial duties using the Marshal's private halls |
