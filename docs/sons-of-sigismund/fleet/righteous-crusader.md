# The Righteous Crusader

<div class="archive-page-label">Flagship · Strike cruiser · Mobile fortress-monastery</div>

<span class="archive-status">Founding vessel established</span> <span class="archive-status open">Interior remains a working design</span>

*The Righteous Crusader* is an existing Black Templars strike cruiser assigned to the founding Marshal through High Marshal Helbrecht's authority. It is no mere transport. It serves the Sons of Sigismund as **warship, home, chapel, archive, reliquary and instrument of rapid assault**.

<div class="archive-quote">A fortress built to travel. A monastery built to fight.</div>

The household's core is imagined as a fortress-monastery within the greater mass of a voidship, its chambers inhabited by Astartes and sustained by a much larger mortal crew. Great Gothic halls stand alongside magazines, hangars, armored bulkheads and plasma machinery.

## Mission and character

The cruiser is expected to deploy warriors, recover imperiled relics, move allied detachments and withdraw to preserve irreplaceable cargo when necessary. The working doctrine favors operational reach and controlled extraction rather than gratuitously risking the vessel in prolonged ship-to-ship battles.

The founding refit takes place **in or near the Armageddon theater**. The exact dockyard, duration, hull dimensions, total crew and precise systems remain unconfirmed.

## Command, worship and memory

Three spaces must be kept separate: the **Grand Reclusiam** is for worship and mass assembly; the **Grand Strategium** is for operational military planning; and the **bridge** is the working voidship command center. The Marshal's private Council Chamber is a **fourth**, more intimate room.

The six sworn honor guards quarter in the Marshal's protected household, near the Inner Sanctum, rather than among ordinary veteran residences.
