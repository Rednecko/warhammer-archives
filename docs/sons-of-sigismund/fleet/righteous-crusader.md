# The Righteous Crusader

**Classification:** Black Templars strike cruiser  
**Function:** Flagship, mobile fortress-monastery and reliquary  
**Home port:** None; fleet-based  
**Initial refit:** In or near the Armageddon theater

An existing Black Templars warship assigned to the founding Marshal under Helbrecht's authority. Its warfighting facilities were adapted to support relic archives, reliquaries, training and the household's close-knit fighting strength.

## Working deck concepts

- **Sanctum of Sigismund:** Grand Reclusiam, Hall of Blades, Hall of Vigil, Strategium and secure Inner Sanctum.
- **Brotherhood and allied deck:** Crusader brotherhood quarters, veteran rooms, Grand Brotherhood Hall, training spaces and separated allied facilities.
- **Grand hangar and logistics:** Thunderhawk operations, drop-pod facilities, motor pool, Apothecarion, forge and relic reception.
- **Crew and engineering:** Separate ship crew, technical support and drives.

These are **provisional design concepts**, not a finalized audited deck plan. Exact room counts, capacities and vehicle complements remain subject to review against the separate ship walkthrough document.
