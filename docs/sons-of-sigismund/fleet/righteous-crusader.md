# 7.1 The Righteous Crusader — Flagship and Fortress-Monastery

<div class="archive-page-label">Sons of Sigismund · Existing Black Templars strike cruiser · Main ship dossier</div>

<span class="archive-status">Vessel and role established</span> <span class="archive-status open">Deck geometry and vessel inventory are working plans</span>

***The Righteous Crusader*** is an **existing Black Templars strike cruiser** entrusted to the Sons of Sigismund by authority of High Marshal Helbrecht in connection with their founding around the Armageddon theater. It was **assigned and refitted**, not built from scratch by the small new household. For the Sons, the vessel is **warship, monastery, living quarters, headquarters, training ground, archive, reliquary and the means of taking their crusade from one threatened world to the next**. It is not simply the box that carries their miniatures into battle: its architecture is intended to express everything their Marshal believes about faith, memory, duty and brotherhood.

Her commanders value the ability to **deploy quickly, retrieve people and relics, and withdraw safely with irreplaceable cargo**. Although formidable in battle, she is not invulnerable; her mission does not require seeking unnecessary prolonged void engagements. Within the armored hull, the atmosphere is a deliberate collision of Imperial Gothic sanctity and working machinery: black-and-white crosses, devotional parchment, iron ribs, bulkheads, bronze, crimson, candlelit stone-like interiors and **yellow standards honoring Sigismund's Imperial Fists heritage**. The order's most treasured artifacts can be seen as part of its purpose, but are not casually exposed to visitors or carried through open cargo spaces.

The ship is **far larger than the Sons of Sigismund themselves**. A compact Astartes fortress-monastery sits within the operations of a huge mortal crew and their service network. The **three principal Space Marine levels** are the **Sanctum of Sigismund**, the **Brotherhood Deck** and the **Grand Hangar and Logistics Deck**. A high-forward **bridge/command complex** lies above or ahead of them; permanent **mortal habitation** extends below and throughout the hull; **engineering and drives** occupy deeper and sternward regions. Thus the six entries below are **a top-to-bottom walkthrough of functional deck regions, not a claim that the entire strike cruiser has exactly six literal floors**.

## The ship at a glance

| Region | Principal role | What must not be confused |
|---|---|---|
| **Upper Bridge** | Actual ship operations under the Shipmaster | Not the Grand Strategium or private council |
| **Sanctum** | Worship, leadership planning, records, relic security | The Hall of Blades is **not** everyday sparring |
| **Brotherhood Deck** | Living, training, common hall and upper embarkation | Planned berths ≠ serving or sworn force strength |
| **Hangar / Logistics** | Landing, cargo, two mass-combat arenas, medical, forge, relic intake | Four planned Thunderhawk positions across **two elevations** |
| **Mortal Service Decks** | Permanent crew habitation, worship, messes and support | Allied surge personnel ≠ full vessel's human crew |
| **Engineering** | Actual cruiser machinery and technical departments | The Astartes Techmarine forge ≠ engine rooms |

The design must leave room for a larger future order **without stating that the present household is already that size**. At founding only **two attached Crusader Squads** and the approved permanent cadre are documented; the present organizational intention is **four eventual Crusading Squads of twenty**.

## Major pathways and access

The normal journey from the sacred levels descends through guarded **port/starboard personnel lift banks**, into Brotherhood residences and finally toward the massive hangar. The **Reclusiam's two rear doors** reach the **Grand Strategium**, whose **guarded rear arch** controls access to the **Inner Sanctum**; its **dedicated upward command lift** leads to the bridge. Shipmaster's personnel use their **own flanking command corridors**, not this private route.

A brother can walk from barracks to a squad armory, sparring room, large training arena or upper flight gallery without crossing the cargo yard. A relic enters by a **secure reception and quarantine route** beside the Techmarine forge and proceeds to archives or the High Relic Vault only after authorization. The mortal crew and allied forces have distinct pathways.

## Refitting and the outstanding blueprints

The founding refit is planned in or near **Armageddon**, adding or adapting a mobile fortress-monastery and protected reliquary to an existing strike cruiser. **The exact dockyard, work duration, hull measurements, literal deck numbering, ship silhouette, total permanent crew, exact weapons, aircraft assignment, final vehicle inventory and all precise geometry remain open.** This wiki is a detailed **walkthrough and room inventory** intended to support eventual art, deck-by-deck maps and ship schematics—not a claim that a final blueprint already exists.
