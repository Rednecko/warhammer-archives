# 7.7 Engineering, Drives and Shipwide Machinery

<div class="archive-page-label">The Righteous Crusader · Deep and aft operational regions</div>

<span class="archive-status">General functional region recorded</span> <span class="archive-status open">Precise equipment, deck plan and ship specification open</span>

Deeper in the cruiser and toward its **stern** lie the engineering spaces that make *The Righteous Crusader* an Imperial warship rather than a collection of halls. Plasma propulsion, major power systems, environmental machinery, shipwide maintenance and reactor control are the responsibility of the **ship's permanent technical population**, not a handful of Astartes. The exact physical arrangement, drive specification, armament, machinery dimensions and deck numbering have never been fixed.

This article describes the **deep engineering region** as a group of decks and protected compartments rather than inventing a single neat floor. It is connected to the mortal service network and industrial cargo routes above, but not placed under the Marshal's chapel as if all machinery were one room.

## 7.7.1 Plasma drives and propulsion spaces

The sternward machinery compartments support the vessel's primary propulsion and related power distribution. These are enormous technical systems with required watchkeeping, maintenance, safety and access controls. The original design specifies the **function and deeper/aft location**, but **does not specify engine count, reactor pattern, drive thrust or exact room geometry**.

**Connection:** Stern engineering work corridors ↔ crew/watch stations ↔ protected shipwide service and maintenance network. Their vertical position relative to the named Astartes levels is broad rather than literally charted.

## 7.7.2 Reactor and power control

Major power and reactor operations support ship systems and ordinary military operations. The working archive treats these as shipwide **engineering departments**; it provides no reactor-room map, named Chief Enginseer, power rating or precisely located safety systems.

**Connection:** Protected technical crew corridors ↔ reactor and power supervision ↔ power distribution and command communications. Crew access to this region does not require entry into Brotherhood barracks.

## 7.7.3 Life support and environmental machinery

The entire ship depends on environmental systems maintaining air, water, sanitation and habitability for the Astartes, embarked allied personnel and permanent mortal crew. These functions exist throughout the hull, with deeper major machinery zones, but the source gives **no signed-off equipment counts or deck-by-deck diagrams**.

**Connection:** Shipwide service galleries ↔ environmental systems ↔ distributed life-support stations near populated decks. The exact plant routing remains an engineering draft issue.

## 7.7.4 Workshops, munitions and protected magazines

Shipboard technical workshops, weapons-support compartments and magazines are distributed according to armament and safety requirements. Dangerous warheads and ammunition are kept **segregated**, not in casual proximity to barracks or devotional areas.

**Connection:** Protected ammunition handling routes and freight systems ↔ shipboard weapons, magazines and relevant servicing areas. This network is distinct from the ordinary pedestrian corridors and from the **Techmarine's forge** on the Hangar and Logistics Deck.

## 7.7.5 Techmarine forge versus ship engineering

The Sons' **Techmarine's forge** maintains Astartes equipment, vehicles and delicate recovered technical relics up on the **Logistics Deck**. The mortal and Mechanicus-operated **main engineering complex** operates the cruiser itself. These two operations work together when necessary, but they are **not the same department, room or location**.

## 7.7.6 Boundaries and future ship schematics

The final floor maps will need to establish engine access, maintenance transit, freight routing, personnel and watch locations, and the relationship between engineering and ship weapons. For now, the source supports **function, general location, controlled access and separation from the Astartes sanctuary**; it does not support an exact sequence of numbered machinery decks.
