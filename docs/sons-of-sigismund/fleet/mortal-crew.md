# 7.6 Mortal Crew and Service Decks

<div class="archive-page-label">The Righteous Crusader · Human habitation and shipwide services</div>

<span class="archive-status">Multideck functional region established</span> <span class="archive-status open">Crew complements, deck counts and watch schedules open</span>

The cruiser is the home not only of a small Black Templars household but of an **immense supporting mortal population**. Below and around the three principal Astartes levels stretch the workaday regions of bridge officers, ratings, Chapter serfs, watchmen, technical specialists, Mechanicus personnel and servitors. Their labor keeps the cruiser alive in transit and in battle. This crew does **not** live inside the Sword Brethren's rooms, and their everyday routes do not cross the brotherhood's sacred chambers.

In the ship design, the principal mortal accommodation and service region lies **below the main cargo and hangar level**, with smaller quarters near critical stations distributed throughout the hull. This is explicitly **a multideck region**, not a single magically spacious floor. The exact number of people and literal deck assignments remain unconfirmed.

## 7.6.1 Permanent crew quarters

Long residential corridors and subdivided watch accommodation provide sleeping, sanitation, storage and ordinary privacy suited to ship officers and ratings. Chapter serfs and security attendants may have separate accommodations or working stations depending on duty. They do not automatically share the living suites of Astartes.

**Connections:** Independent **crew corridor and lift network** ↔ habitation ↔ shipboard workstations, bridge approaches, servicing and engineering regions. Exact local blocks and access-control layouts remain open.

## 7.6.2 Crew messes, recreation and ordinary life

The permanent human population requires its own **mess halls, drinking water, sanitary systems, social and recreation spaces**, distinct from the Great Brotherhood Hall. These functions are part of the approved shipwide services concept, but the draft does **not** specify named halls, precise timetables or particular recreational rituals.

**Connections:** Crew habitation and local duty corridors ↔ mess and service nodes; secure service lifts convey supplies without intruding through the Sanctum or Brotherhood barracks.

## 7.6.3 Shipboard shrines and spiritual service

Human crew require places for devotion as well as places to work. The source establishes **crew shrines and ordinary religious services** in their own quarters, separate from the **Grand Reclusiam** used for major Astartes ceremonies. The identity and number of mortal chaplains or priests, if any, are open.

**Connections:** Crew residential routes ↔ local shrines; any authorized visit to major upper sacred rooms is by controlled route, not open access.

## 7.6.4 Human medical care

Mortal medical services include local support throughout the crew zone, with the principal **Mortal Medicae Wing** situated closer to the **hangar and allied casualty routes** on the logistics level. This provides a practical place for evacuations without routing sick and injured humans into the Astartes Apothecarion.

**Connections:** Local crew facilities ↔ normal medical/service network ↔ principal Mortal Medicae via protected lifts and corridors. The exact individual transfer stations remain unmapped.

## 7.6.5 Stores, workshops and security

Long deployments depend on routine industrial maintenance and stores, local guard posts, small repair facilities, ration distribution and proper handling of consumables. The **main cargo and motor pool above** handle large loads, while crew areas maintain local supply and maintenance routes.

**Connections:** Crew service passages ↔ cargo/freight lifts ↔ shipwide workshops and equipment locations. Access gates separate crew movement from Astartes living quarters, high-security archives and relic chambers.

## 7.6.6 Allied troops are not the permanent crew

The outer allied wings on the **Brotherhood Deck** can accommodate visiting Astra Militarum formations, with a **working 200-person-per-wing capacity** and proposed **500-person surge** elsewhere. A possible deployment model of **300 Guardsmen and 200 supporting personnel** is **not** a standing garrison and **not** the thousands of crew and servitors that might be required for a large Imperial cruiser.

The **173rd Armored Rangers** are a proposed allied formation for potential future shared lore, not proof that they have already embarked or fought alongside the Sons.

## 7.6.7 A mortal watchman's route

An off-duty rating leaves the work station by the ordinary crew passage, passes controlled service security, visits his assigned mess or habitation and returns to a scheduled watch through the crew lift system. The bridge officers have their own command approach above; maintenance teams can reach working machinery and industrial cargo from their service network. The ship is designed to be sustained **without the permanent crew parading through sacred Astartes living quarters**.

!!! warning "Open personnel record"
    The permanent crew population is **not fixed**. The architectural volume suggests that an Imperial strike cruiser may have many thousands of human crew and servitors, but we have approved **no numerical complement**, officer roster, duty schedule or exact internal civic structure.
