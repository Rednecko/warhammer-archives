# 7.2 Upper Command and Bridge Complex

<div class="archive-page-label">The Righteous Crusader · Uppermost command region · Working ship walkthrough</div>

<span class="archive-status">Location and roles defined</span> <span class="archive-status open">Literal deck numbers and room dimensions open</span>

The **bridge complex** is the highest command region in our walkthrough, set **high and forward in the armored command superstructure** of *The Righteous Crusader*, above or forward of the Sanctum of Sigismund. Its upper command dais can be **throne-like and imposing**, but this is still a functioning voidship bridge, not a ceremonial room detached from operations. Here, under the authority of the Marshal, the human **Shipmaster**, his bridge officers, servitors and specialist crew keep a vast warship moving, receiving intelligence, communicating, and coordinating its weapons and flight operations.

The working plan does not claim that all bridge functions fit on one literal floor. This article follows the **upper command region** as a coherent destination. The precise bridge geometry, deck designation, officer names and complete personnel are **unconfirmed**.

## 7.2.1 The main bridge

The bridge is a protected, hard-working operational chamber built around the Shipmaster's commanding station. Helm and navigation consoles, auspex and sensor displays, vox and strategic communications, ship weapons, engineering-status readouts, flight-control positions and servitor stations surround the ship's command core. The design archive suggests **approximately 25–40 mortal and servitor personnel during major operations** as a *planning estimate*, not a recorded complement.

**New working visual direction, approved for the design draft:** Think of the imposing command spaces seen in *Space Marine 2* and animated Warhammer stories. A **raised Marshal's throne-like command station**, watched by a small guard presence, overlooks **banks of working mortal crew consoles** and a **large orbital/battlefield holodisplay**. Ahead is an immense, **reinforced panoramic viewport** framing space, a planet's orbit or the battle outside. The Shipmaster and bridge staff retain their own operational posts rather than becoming decorative attendants to the Marshal. Massive Gothic ribs, armor glazing and severe black-and-white heraldry blend the military bridge with its sacred identity.

The bridge holodisplay gives commanders a **live operational picture**; it does not replace the **Grand Strategium's separate central hololithic planning table** on the Sanctum level. The exact shape of the raised dais, bridge guard detail, console banks, viewport size and window protections are **visual concepts to settle with future art and maps**.

**Connections:** Protected **port and starboard flanking corridors descend from the upper command region to the VIP/liaison wings at the sides of the Sanctum**, forming the ordinary bridge approach for the Shipmaster's watch and authorized visitors. They **do not** send mortal crews through the Grand Reclusiam, Grand Strategium or Marshal's private rooms. A separate **Astartes-rated, guarded command lift descends directly from the bridge complex to the Grand Strategium within the Sanctum**, letting the Marshal, Castellan and authorized Space Marines bypass the guest approaches. The exact landings and security vestibules remain to be drawn.

## 7.2.2 Shipmaster and bridge officers

The Shipmaster commands the routine conduct of the vessel: helm, navigation and shipboard watches, weapons coordination, engineering reports, flight movements and crew responses. During a military emergency the **Marshal or Castellan** may come to the bridge, while the ship's ordinary officers continue their specialized tasks. This is a distinct division of labor, not a second military company command system.

**Connections:** The Shipmaster's own command area opens into the main bridge and its normal officer circulation. The engineering, flight, astropathic or other communications connections are *functional systems and routes*, not finalized adjacent rooms. No individual Shipmaster has been named yet.

## 7.2.3 Navigator's protected domain

The **Navigator** possesses separate, protected quarters and navigation facilities within the broader upper command infrastructure. A Navigator is not simply another helmsman on the bridge. The exact Navigator suite, guard boundaries and relationship to the bridge's physical footprint have not yet been plotted.

**Connection:** Authorized ship-command corridors and controlled communications link the Navigator's domain to bridge operations. The source establishes this **relationship**, not a particular door or floor-plan dimension.

## 7.2.4 Two entrances and one clear distinction

| Access route | Users | What it reaches |
|---|---|---|
| **Port and starboard flanking approaches** | Shipmaster, mortal bridge watch and authorized visitors | Bridge corridors ↔ VIP/liaison wings on either side of the Sanctum |
| **Dedicated Astartes command lift** | Marshal, Castellan and specifically authorized Space Marines | Bridge ↔ Grand Strategium inside the Sanctum, without using VIP wing routes |
| **Protected service network** | Authorized repair and crew personnel | Ship-command support systems; detailed routing open |

The **Grand Strategium is not the bridge**. The Strategium is where Astartes commanders plan a campaign around a hololith; the bridge actually runs the cruiser. The **private Council Chamber** in the Marshal's Inner Sanctum is a third, smaller venue for confidential leadership discussion. Training Command and Control galleries are yet another function, located down on the Brotherhood Deck.

## 7.2.5 A command emergency — walkthrough

The watch receives reports and maintains immediate control of the ship. The Marshal, already working in the **Grand Strategium**, reviews the hololithic picture and may descend toward the Reclusiam to address the brotherhood, pass through the guarded arch into the Inner Sanctum, or take the **dedicated lift upward** into the bridge command complex. Meanwhile the Shipmaster's ordinary crew continues to enter by their own approaches; the cruiser never depends on the assembled Space Marines to perform basic navigation.

## 7.2.6 Names, guard posts and future artwork

**Personnel still to name:** the Shipmaster, the Navigator, the senior mortal bridge officers and any permanent command guard assignments. No character name has been filled in just to occupy a post.

**Floor-plan and illustration notes:** raised Marshal's command dais/seat, Shipmaster's station, orbit-facing panoramic armored viewport, large but functional combat holodisplay, banks of crew consoles, small on-duty guard positions, separate port/starboard guest-command approaches, and guarded direct Astartes lift down to the Strategium. These are **working visual choices**, not precise engineering measurements or newly commissioned personnel.
