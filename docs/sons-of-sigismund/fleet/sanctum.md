# 7.3 Sanctum of Sigismund

<div class="archive-page-label">The Righteous Crusader · Principal sacred and command level</div>

<span class="archive-status">Major sequence and adjacencies established in design draft</span> <span class="archive-status open">Exact dimensions and lesser passages open</span>

Descending from the upper command complex, one reaches the **Sanctum of Sigismund**, the solemn center of the Sons' devotion, memory and leadership. This is the largest **ceremonial** concentration of Astartes facilities on the ship, combining the public grandeur of a gathering cathedral with the layered security of a fortress. Black-and-white crosses, banners honoring Sigismund, yellow references to his Imperial Fists heritage, crimson devotional cloth, iron, bronze and armored Gothic arches establish a distinctly Black Templars character.

The Sanctum is **not an open museum**. Ordinary brothers may enter its gathering places and sacred side halls when appropriate, but access becomes progressively tighter through the Strategium and into the Marshal's Inner Sanctum and High Relic Vault. VIP quarters and command corridors lie to the **flanks**, not inside the central restricted household.

## 7.3.1 Main approach, grand lifts and ceremonial threshold

Principal personnel lifts rise along **both port and starboard sides of the grand hangar**, rather than one central shaft through its middle. The Sanctum-facing arrival points overlook operations through an **armored upper observation gallery**, more ornate than the utilitarian lower landings. Yellow heraldry and a protected speaking position let a Chaplain or commander address Marines preparing to embark below. This is an **expedient hangar address**, not a replacement for the Grand Reclusiam's ordinary sermons.

From the lift/arrival network, a broad armored Gothic entrance leads toward the **Grand Reclusiam**, with the monumental Sigismund statue at the far ceremonial focus. The detailed placement of every passage between the elevator banks and front doors is still a working geometry.

**Connections:** Twin personnel lift banks ↔ Sanctum observation positions ↔ formal Gothic entrance ↔ Grand Reclusiam. On both **port and starboard**, flanking controlled passages connect the Sanctum's **VIP/liaison wings upward to the bridge/upper command region**. Separately, the leadership's **guarded Astartes lift** connects the **Grand Strategium directly upward to the bridge**, without passing through the guest rooms.

## 7.3.2 Grand Reclusiam — heart of worship

The first great chamber is the **Grand Reclusiam**. It is designed to hold roughly **160 power-armored warriors** standing in formation, with attendants and approved guests as appropriate—a *planning capacity*, not the current population of the household. Its wide open floor avoids permanent blocks of pews. Structural pillars, ribbed vaults, banners and the looming image of **Sigismund** give it the gravity of a fortress cathedral. Devotional imagery of the Emperor and Rogal Dorn belongs in the larger sacred ensemble.

The Reclusiam hosts mass, oaths, addresses before major operations, formal remembrance and communal witness to sacrifice. It is neither the ship's bridge nor the Marshal's operational war room.

**Connections:** Its **main entrance** leads back to the grand lifts; its **port side door** goes to the Hall of Blades; its **starboard side door** goes to the Hall of Vigil. At the *rear*, **two guarded doors flank the pulpit and statue** and lead to the **Grand Strategium**. The latter placement is a deliberate improvement over an older draft, where the war room sat farther back.

## 7.3.3 Port side — Hall of Blades

Through the left/port sacred side entrance is the **Hall of Blades**: a consecrated dueling chamber for formal martial examination, instruction and the **Trial of Swords**. A prospective Sword Brother faces his mentor and other veterans here under ritual supervision. The space is intimate enough that the formality and judgment of the duel are unmistakable.

**Connections:** Direct sacred-side access from the **Grand Reclusiam**, under controlled admission. It is **not connected as an ordinary walk-through** to the everyday squad sparring rooms, and it is not one of the two enormous training arenas farther below. Any service or emergency passage is not yet mapped.

## 7.3.4 Starboard side — Hall of Vigil

Across the Reclusiam lies the **Hall of Vigil**, its stillness deliberately contrasting with the Hall of Blades. It is the chamber of lying in state, solemn vigil, private mourning, homage, recovery of the fallen brother's arms and remembrance of his deeds. The personal sacrifice of mortal Imperial defenders may also be honored within the order's records, but the Hall's precise ceremonies have not yet been written.

**Connections:** Direct sacred-side access from the **Grand Reclusiam** on the **starboard**. Its connection to any funerary processing or lower ship medicae route remains **unspecified**, rather than being assumed to cut through the Reclusiam during emergencies.

## 7.3.5 Grand Strategium — the operational war room

Behind the Reclusiam's **paired guarded rear doors** is the **Grand Strategium**, somewhat smaller than the great chapel. A **central hololithic tactical table** dominates this room, surrounded by communications terminals, campaign records, data-slates, honors and planning equipment. Here the Marshal, Castellan and chosen officers examine theaters of war, assault plans, fleet dispositions, relic-recovery intelligence and cooperating Imperial forces.

Authorized allied commanders can attend specific briefings without gaining unrestricted access to the Marshal's residential sanctum. The war room must remain practical, not merely a ceremonial display.

**Connections:** Forward ↔ **two guarded Reclusiam doors**; upward ↔ **dedicated command lift** to the bridge complex; sideways ↔ **controlled VIP/liaison access**; rearward ↔ **one guarded central Gothic arch into the Inner Sanctum**. These are *different security thresholds*, not one open series of corridors for anyone to wander.

## 7.3.6 Guarded archway and the Inner Sanctum

A central Gothic arch beyond the Strategium's hololith marks a second, stricter boundary. Permission to attend a briefing in the Strategium **does not** authorize entry. Past this gate lies the compact personal heart of the order.

### Port residential and archive passage

The **Marshal's chambers and study**, his **household guard station**, the **Castellan's proposed quarters**, and the **Grand Archives/Data Repository** occupy this side. The **six personal Sword Brethren bodyguards**, conceptualized as Bladeguard veterans in the ship design, are quartered **here with the Marshal's Household**. They are **not** counted among the thirty general veteran chambers on the Brotherhood Deck.

The **Grand Archives** hold records and reference material requiring restricted access; they are different from the publicly browsable books and data-slates of the Great Brotherhood Hall. A **private archive lift** connects to supporting record storage elsewhere on the ship, although its lower landing is not final.

**Connections:** Inner Sanctum central passage ↔ Marshal's suite/study, guard station, Castellan quarters and Grand Archives; Grand Archives ↔ dedicated restricted records lift.

### Starboard chaplaincy and private council passage

The **Chaplaincy rooms** and **High Chaplain's contemplated chambers** occupy the opposite side, along with the Marshal's **private Council Chamber** and a **Private Arsenal**. A Terminator-armored **High Chaplain** is a working character concept, not proof of a separately named founding appointment.

The **Council Chamber** handles confidential leadership deliberation, as distinct from the Strategium's large hololithic military briefings. The **Private Arsenal** is for the immediate household, not the logistics deck's industrial forge and general stores.

**Connections:** Inner Sanctum central passage ↔ Chaplaincy, proposed High Chaplain's rooms, Council Chamber, Private Arsenal.

## 7.3.7 The High Relic Vault

At the far, most tightly controlled end of the Inner Sanctum stands the **High Relic Vault**, preserving the most sensitive and worthy objects. The final approach narrows by access privilege as much as by physical architecture. Recovered artifacts **do not come directly from the hangar** to this vault: they are received, documented, quarantined when required and examined within the lower logistics complex first. Less sensitive approved material may be stored in the Grand Archives or other protected collections instead.

**Connections:** Restricted Inner Sanctum approach ↔ High Relic Vault; separate controlled archive and approved relic-transfer systems (exact freight route not established). The vault has **no unrestricted public entrance**.

## 7.3.8 VIP and command-liaison wings

**Port and starboard flanking guest wings** belong on this broad Sanctum level, **outside** the Inner Sanctum. A handful of austere private suites, small meeting rooms and reception spaces accommodate allied commanders, visiting Astartes officers and Imperial dignitaries. Their normal routes rise toward the upper bridge complex via **controlled command passages** and give invited guests a supervised approach to the Strategium.

**Connections:** Both **port and starboard** guest/liaison corridors ↔ ordinary rising bridge-command approaches, and ↔ authorized Strategium access for a specifically invited council attendee. The dedicated **Astartes command lift** is instead reached from the **Grand Strategium itself**. Neither the guest corridors nor their bridge access lead freely into the Marshal's private quarters, High Relic Vault, Chaplaincy or archives.

## 7.3.9 A walk through the sacred level

An Initiate rises from the Brotherhood Deck, crosses the grand threshold and enters the Reclusiam among his brothers. Looking left, he knows the Hall of Blades is where a worthy candidate might earn the right to stand among the Sword Brethren; to his right, the Hall of Vigil holds the memory of those who will never return. During a campaign council the Marshal passes **behind the pulpit through the paired doors** into the Strategium, where the hololith waits. Past its guarded arch lies the Inner Sanctum—the private home of the Marshal's household and the safe approach to the order's greatest relics.

!!! warning "Continuity checks"
    The **Grand Strategium is behind the Reclusiam**, *before* the guarded Inner Sanctum. The **bridge is above/forward** and the **private Council Chamber is inside** the Inner Sanctum. The Hall of Blades, small squad sparring halls and two main arenas all serve **different** roles. Every capacity is an architectural target, not a founding troop count.
