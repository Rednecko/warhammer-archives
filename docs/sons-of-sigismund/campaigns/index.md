## The first crusade: Armageddon

The Sons of Sigismund begin in the continuing war for Armageddon, with orders to support the Imperial war effort and defend or recover threatened heritage.

The founding Marshal's earlier killing of a regional Ork Warboss is **pre-commission history**. It must never be counted as the Sons of Sigismund's first tabletop victory.

<div class="archive-strip"><strong>Awaiting first engagement.</strong> The order has not yet fought a recorded post-founding battle. Its opponents, result, casualties, promotions and recovered relics are not predetermined.</div>
