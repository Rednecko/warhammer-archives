# Armageddon: two distinct histories

<div class="archive-page-label">Theater dossier · Continuity boundary</div>

<span class="archive-status">Pre-founding history established</span> <span class="archive-status open">Post-founding campaign unplayed</span>

Two very different phases of the order's story take place in the Armageddon theater. Keeping them separate is essential to the battle chronicle.

## Before the founding: the Sword Brother's campaign

The future Marshal, still a veteran Sword Brother, rallied failing defenders with a recovered standard, the relic sword and sustained battlefield leadership. His Initiates, Neophytes and Imperial comrades helped carry those efforts across the front.

After weeks of preparation, he forced an original regional Ork Warboss into a public duel and killed him. The resulting disorder gave Imperial defenders the opportunity to reclaim a **significant local sector** through prolonged counterattacks. **This did not conclude the planetary war.**

These are *established backstory events*, occurring **before** the Sons of Sigismund existed as a commissioned household.

## After the founding: the order's first crusade

High Marshal Helbrecht's new household prepares its initial deployment from *The Righteous Crusader* in the continuing Armageddon war. The first assignment is to fight the Emperor's enemies while protecting and recovering threatened Imperial heritage.

**No post-founding battle has yet been played or recorded.** The first tabletop engagement will establish the first proper Sons of Sigismund entry in the campaign chronicle.

## Deliberately unresolved

The exact Armageddon sector, campaign dating, Warboss's name, individual unit names and interactions with named official Armageddon campaigns require further decisions. They should be aligned with current published material before being presented as precise dates or crossovers.
