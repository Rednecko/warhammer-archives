# Sources and editorial notes

<div class="archive-page-label">Behind the archive · Provenance</div>

## Primary source: collaborative lore

This is a **fan-created Black Templars crusading household**, developed through iterative decisions, tabletop collection planning and original vessel design. Its story is not presented as official Games Workshop canon.

The wiki was assembled from the project's working **Sons of Sigismund — Chapter Creation & Organization** Google Doc and these internal development archives:

- *Sons of Sigismund — Founding Lore Archive*, 18 November 2026;
- *Sons of Sigismund — Organization Dossier*, working versions 1.0–1.6;
- *The Righteous Crusader — Volume I*, architectural and operational working draft, 17 November 2026.

These files serve as **private writing and development records**; this public page does not grant access to the originals.

## When sources disagree

The **latest explicitly approved user decision takes precedence** over older drafts. In particular, the founding history now places the Sword Brother's Ork campaign on Armageddon, makes Helbrecht the initiator of his commission, and establishes **ten** founding Sword Brethren, not the larger planning strengths found in earlier documents.

Detailed ship spaces remain valuable, but many counts are **working capacity concepts** and must not be treated as the actual original army muster.

## The tabletop record

Actual Warhammer 40,000 games will become the official story of the household's adventures **after its founding**. Until games have been played, battle outcomes remain blank.

## Official universe and ownership

Warhammer 40,000, the Black Templars, Sigismund, Helbrecht and related official settings are associated with Games Workshop. This is an **unofficial fan-made narrative wiki**, not affiliated with or endorsed by Games Workshop. Custom characters, campaigns, ship arrangements and relic descriptions are homebrew material.

**Reference priority:** approved project decisions → recent continuity register → separately reviewed development archives → official Warhammer background where relevant → optional community inspiration. Community sources are not automatically canon.
