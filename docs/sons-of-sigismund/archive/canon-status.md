# Continuity register

<div class="archive-page-label">Editorial archive · Single source of truth</div>

The wiki is a living narrative. Status labels prevent old drafts, proposed figures and actual played battles from being conflated.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Established** | User-approved direction or explicit decision |
| **Working** | A developed concept retained for review, not immutable canon |
| **Open** | Specific information not yet decided |
| **Superseded** | Older draft replaced by a later explicit decision |
| **Recorded** | Actual played battle or separately approved event |

## Established foundations

- The Sons of Sigismund are a **Black Templars crusading household**, not a separate successor Chapter.
- High Marshal Helbrecht commissioned the founding Marshal following his **pre-commission Armageddon campaign**.
- The founder was a distinguished Sword Brother; he **did not petition Helbrecht** for promotion.
- The recovered **relic sword** and **battle standard** were pivotal in his sustained morale campaign and public duel against an **original regional Ork Warboss**.
- The duel enabled, rather than magically completed, a significant **Imperial regional victory**.
- The order's flagship is the strike cruiser ***The Righteous Crusader***, functioning as mobile fortress-monastery and reliquary.
- **Ten** founding Sword Brethren: six honor guards, two Crusader Squad veterans, one household duelist and one held in reserve for a later squad.
- Original founding command includes a **Marshal, Emperor's Champion and Chaplain**; two attached Crusader Squads are **not yet permanently sworn**.
- Permanent membership comes from an **Oath of Allegiance**, distinguished from the **Trial of Swords** and relic-specific custodianship vows.
- Sword Brethren gain an **optional, permanent yellow-heraldry privilege** through their earned standing.
- The **Litany of the Blade** is established; its final line is used **only at induction**.
- The order's current fighting core is **close-knit and concentrated**, with its Marshal leading personally from the front.
- **Zero post-founding tabletop battles have been recorded.**

## Working / proposed material

- High Chaplain in Terminator armor, closely involved in frontline sermons and reliquary rites;
- Castellan and wider command details;
- Extensive three-zone ship plan, including four future Brotherhood complexes, two combat arenas, planned hangar complements and allied accommodation;
- Longer-term increase in household strength;
- Guiding phrase **“Beacon of Faith and Wrath”** as possible formal motto;
- Oath of Custodianship wording and commemorative prayers.

## Superseded drafts — do not silently restore

- **Approximately 60 permanently sworn plus 100 rotating warriors** as the founding strength;
- **Twenty permanent Sword Brethren** in the founding cadre;
- The original founder **petitioning Helbrecht** rather than being summoned and offered a commission;
- The founding Ork campaign occurring in an unrelated unnamed system;
- The founding Warboss being an already named official character;
- Treating the founder's pre-commission Ork victory as a battle won by the newly commissioned household.
