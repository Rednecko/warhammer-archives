## Armor of the brotherhood

The order's shared visual language is dark, battle-worn armor with traditional white shoulders and Black Templars crosses. The current painting direction favors a **grimdark, weathered black** finish rather than bright blue edge highlights.

Yellow heraldry commemorates Sigismund and the Imperial Fists legacy, but is neither mandatory nor a universal rank uniform.

## The right of yellow

A warrior gains the privilege upon completing the **Trial of Swords** and becoming a Sword Brother. The privilege remains his for life, including if he later serves as a Chaplain, Castellan, Marshal or in another office. A veteran may choose to use very little yellow, or none; the absence of yellow does not revoke his accomplishment.

Possible personal applications include:

- A yellow pauldron bearing a black cross;
- A yellow cross on a white pauldron;
- A single greave or knee panel;
- A yellow helmet panel or stripe;
- Discreet personal accents.

**Initiates and Neophytes do not wear the earned yellow honor.** No rigid squad-number or helmet-rank color code has been ratified.

## Beyond the armor

Crimson cloth, iron, bronze, devotional parchment, scratched heraldry and relic ornaments can embellish veteran miniatures without displacing the central black-and-white identity. Relic quantity and ornament are **not an automatic measure of rank**.
