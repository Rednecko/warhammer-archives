# Rites, trials and oaths

<div class="archive-page-label">The household's living traditions</div>

The Sons of Sigismund are bound by duty to the Emperor, by their service to the Black Templars and by customs centered on **mentorship, earned excellence and memory**. Their rites are related but distinct: an oath to join the household is not automatically an oath to guard a relic, and the Trial of Swords does not replace either.

## Trial of Swords

A sponsoring Sword Brother mentors an Initiate over years or decades, teaching arms, faith, history and responsibility. The candidate must prove his readiness through formal duels with his mentor and other Sword Brethren. The trial is neither ordinary barracks sparring nor a rapid promotion ceremony; it takes place in the **Hall of Blades** aboard *The Righteous Crusader*.

The successful candidate gains the title of **Sword Brother** and the lifelong, **optional privilege of yellow armor heraldry**. The privilege need not be displayed on every item of armor. The intent of the trial is not merely to train a subordinate, but to raise a warrior worthy to stand as a brother among veterans.

## The Litany of the Blade

<div class="archive-page-label">Approved ceremonial text</div>

> A sword without purpose is but sharpened iron.  
> A warrior without faith is but flesh awaiting death.
>
> I shall break thy pride.  
> I shall sharpen thy will.  
> I shall test thy faith.  
> I shall strip thee of all that makes thee unworthy.
>
> When thy strength fails, let conviction sustain thee.  
> When thy conviction falters, let faith deliver thee.  
> When death stands before thee, let it tremble at thy resolve.
>
> Temper thy steel with purpose.  
> Temper thy soul with devotion.  
> Become the instrument of His divine wrath.
>
> And when thou standest before me, bloodied but unbroken, I shall lay down my blade and call thee brother.

**The final line is spoken only at formal induction**, not during ordinary recitations.

## Oath of Allegiance

The Oath of Allegiance creates **permanent membership** in the Sons of Sigismund. Attached Black Templars can fight beside the household without taking it; they may return to their former crusading forces. The exact words and ceremony remain open.

## The burden of relics

Sword Brethren may be entrusted with individual artifacts and charged to study and protect them. A separate relic-specific vow has been proposed under the **working name Oath of Custodianship**; its final wording is not established.

## Remembrance and the Hall of Vigil

The Hall of Vigil is the ship's working memorial and mourning space. A fallen brother may lie in state while his relics are recovered and their stewardship documented. Further rites—including proposed prayers and final disposition of remains—are still under development.
