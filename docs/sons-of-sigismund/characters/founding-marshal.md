# The Founding Marshal

**Name:** Undecided  
**Rank:** Marshal, Sons of Sigismund  
**Previous rank:** Veteran Black Templars Sword Brother  
**Signature armament:** Founding relic sword; founding standard accompanies him into battle under the care of its bearer.

The Marshal is a severe traditionalist, an exceptionally skilled and fearless swordsman, and a commander who leads personally from the front. He venerates Sigismund's example: duty, sacred oaths, martial discipline, faith and sacrifice.

Normally reserved and economical with words, he can become an explosive, compelling orator at the decisive moment. This ability was instrumental in rallying Imperial defenders during the pre-founding Armageddon campaign.

His household is small and close-knit. He knows and repeatedly fights beside his brothers, rather than governing a distant, dispersed chapter. He demands high standards and shares the dangers he asks them to endure.

**Working characterization:** The severity, frontline leadership and martial tradition are established in direction; his precise response to a brother's failure and his final personal biography remain undecided.
