This checklist compares our actual **Sons of Sigismund** encyclopedia with the **user-supplied Space Marine Chapter Creation template** and the **Black Templars wiki article structure**. The outside references help us identify *topics*, **not authorize made-up lore**. Our subject is a **small Black Templars crusading household**, not a new Codex Chapter, so generic templates must be adapted rather than followed literally.

**Priority of authority:** later explicit user decisions → [our continuity register](../sons-of-sigismund/archive/canon-status.md) → established project dossiers → official Black Templars background → fan-wiki templates. Mark unanswered questions *open*, not *canon*.

## First priority — Finish the actual organization

The **organization chart is sufficiently designed**. We should now answer the questions required for real rosters and realistic duties—not redesign the hierarchy.

- **Marshal and Castellan:** their names, how the Castellan was selected, his authority when the Marshal leads from the front, and the date he formally assumes office. **Do not insert a Castellan into the established founding roster without agreement.**
- **Marshal's Household:** the ten original Sword Brethren are **six guards + two initially alongside the First Crusading Squad + one duelist + one reserve**. Define personal identities, relationships and fighting styles without double-counting.
- **Crusading Squads:** long-term **4 × 20 = 80** Space Marines across the squads, with **one Household Sword Brother in each group of twenty**, and **nineteen Initiates/Neophytes** whose ratio is still open. The first two formations existed as attached Crusader Squads at founding; the Third and Fourth are *future*.
- **Shared support:** determine whether a named Techmarine, Apothecary, Crusade Ancient, reliquary custodian and shipmaster are present **at a specific later stage**, or are still only functions fulfilled through the broader Black Templars.
- **Relationship of command and membership:** are the assigned Sword Brethren always tactical leaders of their squads, or sometimes veteran sponsors serving beside an Initiate squad leader? **No answer yet.**

### Next high-value decision

**What authority does each attached Sword Brother hold over the Crusading Squad?** A confirmed squad command role, or veteran mentorship with operational leadership assigned case by case? This matters before fixing rosters, squad markings and battlefield procedures.

## Second priority — Give the brotherhood a lived identity

**Founder:** develop recruitment world/early life, first crusade, mentors, experiences of failure, and what made him both severe and deeply loyal to ordinary brothers. Preserve the existing Armageddon story as the pre-commission turning point.

**Culture and daily life:** how training, relic scholarship, communal worship, rest and remembrance fit together aboard the ship. Decide how the Marshal handles incompetence versus disobedience, and how the brothers treat **mortal crew and Guardsmen**. These decisions should shape named characters—not become generic boilerplate.

**Rites:** write and approve the exact Oath of Allegiance, decide how it differs visibly from Trial of Swords and relic custodianship, and specify the timing/witnesses/meaning of each ceremony.

## Third priority — Make the wiki visibly recognizable as our army

**Heraldry:** keep the established cross, black plate and white shoulders, plus optional yellow earned by Sword Brethren. Design simple, model-friendly visual identifiers for four squads, veteran appointments and special offices. Do not decree new rank colors until approved.

**Painting:** convert the agreed grimdark black look into a testable painting recipe, then photograph representative Neophyte, Initiate, Sword Brother and Household character miniatures. **Actual photo plates** will be more useful than repeated prose.

**Relics:** name the founding sword and standard, establish their provenance and historical bearers, and record who safeguards them aboard *The Righteous Crusader*.

## Fourth priority — Operational depth and outside relationships

- **Relic missions:** how an artifact is located, authenticated, secured, evacuated, logged, conserved and brought back into service; who decides when preserving history conflicts with an urgent combat duty.
- **The ship:** establish crew and shipmaster, actual launch craft and vehicle inventory, who uses its planned decks, and the difference between physical room **capacity** and actual personnel.
- **Allies:** name only relationships we establish. Respect for Armageddon Guardsmen is part of the founding story; any joint campaign with the **173rd Armored Rangers** is still a proposal.
- **Enemy record:** preserve the pre-commission Ork Warboss as an original regional foe; wait for the first **post-founding tabletop** battle before adding a victory or casualty.

## Generic template fields that do **not** fit our household

An independent Chapter's numbered Founding, successor Chapters, sovereign homeworld, separate gene-seed strain, Codex 10-company establishment and automatic full Chapter command offices are **not missing data** for the Sons of Sigismund. The appropriate answer is **“Not applicable; this is a Black Templars crusading household.”** Nor should we invent a gene-seed defect merely because a random creation table invites one.

The outside Black Templars articles describe much larger crusades and **Castellans leading Fighting Companies**. Our deliberate adaptation is **one Castellan under the Marshal, with four small future Crusading Squads led or mentored by assigned Household Sword Brethren**. That remains compatible with our chosen identity without copying a big crusade's administrative scale.
