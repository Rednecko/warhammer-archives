# Lore development roadmap

<div class="archive-page-label">Williams Warhammer Lore · The workshop</div>

The Sons of Sigismund founding scaffold is **approximately 90% developed**. We are no longer building exclusively in chronological order. The purpose of this list is to focus on the biggest *creative gaps* while keeping the wiki expanding without inventing battles or names prematurely.

## 01 — Character and culture of the household (next)

**Established:** Small, centralized and close-knit; a traditionalist frontline Marshal who is normally reserved and can become an extraordinary orator; original brothers and some new appointments; relics symbolize remembrance and fortitude.

**Still to decide:**

- How the Marshal disciplines a brother who fails, and how a warrior can redeem a mistake;
- Ordinary treatment of mortal Guardsmen, shipmasters, crew and serfs;
- Where the order draws the line between protecting Imperial memory and honoring individual sacrifice;
- Whether veterans have personal rituals or common daily habits beyond the approved Litany.

**First recommended decision:** Does a brother who fails receive immediate severe punishment, a formal chance to redeem himself under rigorous testing, or a distinction based on the nature of his failure?

## 02 — Everyday life aboard The Righteous Crusader

**Working designs:** Grand Reclusiam, Hall of Blades, Hall of Vigil, command Strategium, Inner Sanctum, Brotherhood quarters, communal hall, training arenas, hangars and reliquary workflow.

**To invent with approval:** Typical daily schedule, personal chambers, non-combat recreation, interactions with mortal crew, training and shipboard customs. Architecture does not determine the lived culture by itself.

## 03 — First named characters

Profiles for the founding Chaplain, Emperor's Champion, six honor guards, two embedded veteran mentors and the household duelist. Do not give anyone a name or unplayed triumph without approval. Official Emperor's Champion investiture traditions must inform the character, but his exact origin remains open.

## 04 — Relics, oaths and visible identity

Formal names and provenance for the two founding relics; the bearer of the ancient standard; exact Oath of Allegiance; proposed oath of custodianship; formal warcry and heraldic markings. Yellow remains **earned, optional and permanent** for Sword Brethren.

## 05 — Allies and enemies

Relationship to other Black Templars crusades, the Imperial Guard, Mechanicus, Ecclesiarchy, Inquisition and the potential 173rd Armored Rangers connection. Do not assume a joint battle has occurred.

## 06 — The military organization beyond founding

How the two attached Crusader Squads operate, exact sizes and assignments, when specialists arrive, how sworn members are accepted, who commands small detachments and how much of the ship's capacity is active. Remember: ship capacity is not founding force strength.

## 07 — Campaign chronicle and model record

The first post-founding Armageddon battle is **unplayed**. Once it occurs, record mission, outcome, participating miniatures and lasting consequences using the [battle-report template](../sons-of-sigismund/campaigns/battle-log-template.md).

## Suggested development cycle

1. Talk through **one small decision** in chat.
2. Explicitly label what is agreed and what is only a working suggestion.
3. Add approved content to the most relevant wiki page, with cross-links where useful.
4. Update the continuity record and master working source when a consequential fact changes.
5. Leave all remaining questions open—don't fill them with generic placeholder lore.
