# Lore research library

**Important adaptation:** The Sons of Sigismund are a **Black Templars crusading household**, not a newly founded independent Chapter. Fields such as numbered Founding, Chapter Master, new gene-seed lineage, separate successor chapters and homeworld must not be invented merely to fill a generic template.

## 4. Our primary creative record

The [Sons of Sigismund continuity register](../sons-of-sigismund/archive/canon-status.md) tracks decisions. The original Google Docs and Word development dossiers remain the project's working archives, which are not reproduced wholesale on this public site.

**Decision rule:** official Warhammer setting for constraints → approved user-created lore for our original order → draft possibilities clearly labeled. Neither a fan wiki nor an older project draft automatically overrides explicit decisions.

## 5. Questions raised by outside sources

**The Emperor's Champion:** Official background describes a warrior receiving a vision before battle and being invested by Chaplains. The founding roster includes an Emperor's Champion, but **the identity, visionary experience, when the role was assumed and whether he came with the Marshal remain undecided**. Keep this open rather than silently depicting an arbitrary permanent office.

**Crusader Squads:** Canonical structure commonly mingles Sword Brethren, Initiates and Neophytes. Our founding ten Sword Brethren can have assigned mentoring relationships without treating those slots as additional tabletop models.

**Mortal serfs:** A fleet-based household needs skilled mortal command staff, archives personnel, armor and reliquary attendants, launch crews and ship ratings. Their cultural place and treatment are key unexplored topics.

**Armageddon:** The Ork victory is *original pre-commission backstory*. Official large-scale Armageddon campaigns provide a setting, not permission to invent victories over named canon figures.

## Editorial conventions

- **Established:** explicitly approved original lore;
- **Working:** concept to revisit;
- **Open:** decision not yet made;
- **Superseded:** old draft;
- **Recorded:** played battle or separately approved event.

Always summarize sources in our own words. Don't paste extended copyrighted excerpts, source artwork, or entire books.
